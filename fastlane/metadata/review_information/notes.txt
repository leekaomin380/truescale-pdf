TrueScale PDF does not require an account or external hardware.

To review:
1. Choose "粘贴文本".
2. Paste sample text.
3. Select A4, A5, or B5 and click preview.
4. Save the generated PDF with "另存 PDF".

The "网页链接" feature makes network requests only after the reviewer enters an HTTPS URL and starts conversion. Local documents remain on the Mac. The app has no analytics, advertising, sign-in, purchases, or reader-device delivery integration.

Pandoc, Typst, and GNU MP licenses and corresponding source materials are included in the app under Resources/licenses and are also published with the matching GitHub Release.
